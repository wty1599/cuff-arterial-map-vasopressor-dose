# Analysis chronology

The initial feasibility extraction preceded the primary analysis and was not a confirmatory test of dose-related device performance. The original pairing, dose bands and artifact rules were carried into the primary analysis. A feasibility coverage denominator that included periods without arterial monitoring was withdrawn after the feasibility results were seen; the reason was a mismatch with the paired-measurement estimand, not a change in the paired-analysis cohort.

The primary endpoint was the probability of a cuff MAP at least 65 mmHg conditional on arterial MAP below 65 mmHg. False alarms, agreement summaries and dose analyses were part of the main analysis stage.

After primary results, explanatory analyses added deep hypotension, cuff-conditioned residual probabilities and low-pressure-depth standardization. Population restrictions, catheter-site comparisons and bias by blood-pressure range were subsequently added in response to clinical and literature questions.

The common measurement-relation analysis was added after the earlier dose-stratified results. It assesses compatibility with differing arterial-pressure distributions; it does not establish constant device error or equal accuracy. Common-anchor comparisons of time windows were added later to distinguish window effects from selected-sample effects. The additional cuff threshold and likelihood ratios were added after the earlier manuscript analyses, with rules recorded before their new confidence intervals were examined.

The present repository preparation does not refit these analyses. It adapts paths, removes historical output-count assertions from distributable code, applies disclosure suppression and rounding, and redraws the Brief Report's rate tables and figure. The SICdb bootstrap diagnostic is a later diagnostic of interval behavior; it does not replace the primary estimate or percentile interval.
