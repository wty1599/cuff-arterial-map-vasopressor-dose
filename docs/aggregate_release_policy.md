# Aggregate disclosure policy

The threshold-rate and likelihood-ratio tables distribute rounded proportions and confidence limits. Their patient and pair counts are withheld. The repository does not distribute individual contributions, patient identifiers, timestamps, bootstrap replicates, or source-audit records. Any rate cell whose contributing denominator includes fewer than ten patients is omitted. Likelihood-ratio cells require at least ten reference-positive and ten reference-negative patients. Cells without adequate denominator evidence are also omitted.

The v5 S6 density layer is a specific, author-authorized display exception. It supplies bin centres and aggregate counts only for bins containing at least ten pairs **and** ten distinct patients. Other bins are omitted entirely, including coordinates and counts. Only the selected grid for each database is distributed; alternative grids and unsuppressed bins remain private. Whole-database coverage summaries and existing bias/limits are supplied without patient identifiers or paired observations. See [v5 display preparation](brief_report_v5.md).

Proportions and limits are rounded to 0.001 (0.1 percentage point); likelihood ratios to two decimals. Precision flags and original intervals are retained for releasable cells. The requested operational flag also covers estimates less than one percentage point from a confidence bound, which can include narrow intervals in large samples; the label alone does not demonstrate low precision. These display changes do not alter the original estimates or analytical definitions. The detailed suppression ledger remains in the restricted local audit and is not included here.

Complementary-cell protection: threshold-rate tables omit exact counts, marginal totals, per-patient contribution percentages and full-precision ratios. The S6 exception releases only the selected retained bins, without suppressed-bin coordinates or a second grid permitting subtraction. Model/density outputs without a defensible patient denominator are withheld. Plots and tables use the approved disclosure layers. External manuscript disclosures must be reviewed together with this package before a future public release.

The reconstructed Brief Report tables show the published rate components with the current display rules; they intentionally omit the original count columns. This is not a claim of byte-for-byte reproduction of the manuscript tables. The figure is redrawn from display-precision summaries; uncertainty comes from the original analysis, not from a new fit.
